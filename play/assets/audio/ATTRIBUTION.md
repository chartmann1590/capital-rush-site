# Sound credits

Footsteps, metal impacts, punches and the bus-stop bell are from Kenney's **Impact Sounds**, licensed CC0 (public domain).

Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

The original license is preserved in `License.txt`. `manifest.json` records the verified source archive and hashes of the ten included OGG recordings. Run `npm run ingest:audio` to reproduce the import.

Engines, horn, siren, city ambience and the three radio stations are synthesized by the game. Radio tracks are original procedural instrumental sequences.
