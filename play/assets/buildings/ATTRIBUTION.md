# Architecture detail credits

Six window, door and cornice modules from **Modular Factory Facade**, by **James Ray Cock**, Poly Haven, CC0 1.0 Universal.

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/modular_factory_facade
License: https://polyhaven.com/license

`manifest.json` records every source checksum, runtime checksum and module triangle count. `node tools/fetch-building-details.mjs` reproduces the import. Author geometry and PBR materials are retained; textures are converted to 512px WebP and unused modules removed. These generic modules are not representations of specific Capital Region landmarks.
