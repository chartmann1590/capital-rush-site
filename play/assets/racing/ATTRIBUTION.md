# Racing assets

- `horse.glb`: the Horse from Quaternius' [Ultimate Animated Animal Pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html), CC0 1.0. Trimmed to its gallop, walk and idle clips and pruned with gltf-transform (1.1 MB to 327 KB); the coat colours are tinted in the game.
- `tracks.json`: built by `tools/racing/build_tracks.py` from OpenStreetMap (`data/racing/tracks-osm.json`), © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0.
